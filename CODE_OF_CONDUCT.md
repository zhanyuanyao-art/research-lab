# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and changes, not the person. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, and sharing someone else's private information are not.

Use invented research examples when discussing a problem. Do not post another person's papers, annotations, conversations, credentials, or screenshots without permission. Follow the same standard when using an AI assistant to contribute.

Maintainers may ask for a change, remove harmful content, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Explain decisions privately when that can be done without exposing the reporter or private information.

The maintainer is Leo Yao ([@zhanyuanyao-art](https://github.com/zhanyuanyao-art)). Report a conduct concern privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/zhanyuanyao-art/research-lab/security/advisories/new) with a title starting "Conduct:", or through GitHub's own content reporting. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. There is no reporting email address. Security defects follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
